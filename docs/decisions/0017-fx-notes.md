# 0017 - FX implementation notes

Render/audio only; `packages/shared/src/sim` untouched. Owner pre-approved, so the visual-approval STOP was skipped (screenshots are in `docs/test-results/FX-*.png`).

- **Hit.** Silhouette flash is the existing white tint driven by `Fx.hitFlash[defender]` (1 frame, 2 on strength 2). Spikes 6/9/12 (tips 1.6x longer than before), debris 4/8/14 with gravity (900 px/s^2), shock ring r 8 -> 28/40/56 over 10 f, streak lines along knockback, plus a short cached-glow particle behind the star. `lowFx` or Reduced effects halves spike/debris counts. Knockback direction is "defender x >= attacker x".
- **Particle pool.** Still 256 objects, ring-buffer reuse. New kinds `debris | shock | streak | glow`, new field `r1`. Existing test expectation for a strength-0 hit changed from 7 to 14 live particles.
- **Camera.** Trauma add 0.25/0.45/0.7 by strength (+0.6 super, +0.8 K.O., +0.12 block, a small judgement call), decay 0.05/frame, shake = trauma^2 * 6 px, offsets from `hash01(localTick)` (seeded, render-only). Events count once per sim frame (`lastEventFrame`) so rollback replays cannot re-shake. Punch zoom 1.04 for 6 f on strength 2; K.O. zoom 1.15 eased in over 20 f and out over the last 20 f, focused on the K.O. event position. Zoom wraps the world and FX but not the HUD.
- **Trails.** `Trails.ts` ring buffer of 6 points per fighter in world px, fed from the fist (punch buttons) or foot (kick buttons) tip of the front limb while the move clip is in its active frames; fades one point per render frame afterwards. Skipped under `lowFx`. Points are recorded per render frame (not per sim tick), so on slow displays the strip is shorter in time.
- **Block / parry.** Hex shield (bigger, thicker, glow), small shock ring, 6 chevron sparks (3 under lowFx). Parry: jade ring + shock ring + `saturation` composite overlay for 6 f (0.1 s) between stage and fighters.
- **Dust / dash.** The sim emits no event for jump/dash, so `Fx.observeMotion` compares fighter state names between consumed frames (jumpSquat->air: dust; dashF/dashB entry: dust + 3 streaks). Land event: 6 puffs (halved under lowFx, minimum 4).
- **Super.** 8 f accent flash, 18 radial speed lines rotating about screen centre (10 under lowFx) instead of the old diagonal lines, bloom glows (cached sprites) behind the attacker, existing portrait cut-in kept. HUD "ready" pulse: a 30 f expanding outline when a meter first reaches full.
- **K.O.** 3 white pulses on the loser (existing 18 f counter now drives `hitFlash`), two shock rings, a screen-crack sprite pre-rendered once (30 f), zoom, and the "K.O." banner delayed to 30 f after the K.O. event. The task text says a 40 f slow-mo "exists"; it did not, and the sim must not slow, so the look is: particles advance at half speed while `koLeft > 0`.
- **Combo.** Pop 1.4 -> 1 over 10 f on each new hit, colour white / gold (3+) / red (6+).
- **Audio.** `Cue.rate` (+5 % per combo hit after the first, cap +30 %) is applied by scaling oscillator and filter frequencies in `AudioBus.build`. New `thump` recipe (low-passed noise + falling sub sine) is queued on strength 2 hit/counter/throw.
- **Settings.** `screenShake` (default true) and `reducedFx` (default false) added to `Settings`; reducedFx ORs into `Fx.lowFx`. Missing stored fields fall back to defaults. Shown in a new "Effects" fieldset.
- **Volcano p95 investigation.** Bisected by deleting parts of `volcano.ts` under the 4x throttle: the lava zigzag costs nothing measurable; smoke arcs + embers together cost about 0.6 ms of main-thread time per frame; the rest is cached layer blits. Smoke now uses a cached soft sprite (kept). The p95 gap vs the other stages (about 22 vs 18.5 ms) did not close, and run-to-run noise on this machine is +/- 1.5 ms of p95, so no cheap single fix was found; it is recorded, not resolved.
- `tests/browser/fx-shots.spec.ts` regenerates the four screenshots with `FX_SHOTS=<dir>`; it is skipped otherwise.
